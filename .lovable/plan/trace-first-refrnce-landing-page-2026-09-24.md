5. **What Trace establishes** — Explain transaction provenance, counterparty relationships, asset movement, exposure paths, and decision-ready evidence.
6. **Supporting intelligence** — Retain IST/RQS and other ratings as corroborating context beneath the evidence layer rather than the main product promise.
7. **Institutional workflow fit** — Show how Trace supports research, compliance, risk, and investment-committee review without replacing existing processes.
8. **Access section** — Restyle the existing working request form to match the new page while preserving validation and delivery behavior.
9. **Footer and metadata** — Update the positioning language from broad control-layer/scoring copy to Trace-first messaging.

## Visual and interaction details
- Use semantic tokens for warm canvas, ink, graphite, rules, evidence green, review amber, and alert red.
- Use squared or lightly rounded modules, generous whitespace, fine borders, and no gradients or decorative effects.
- Build the evidence graph in HTML/CSS/SVG as an original interface, not as a copy of the supplied screenshot.
- Add restrained node/line reveals and section fades, with reduced-motion support.
- Stack the system diagram and evidence views clearly on mobile while preserving the asymmetric desktop composition.
